# Typhoon Banking Platform: User Guide

Welcome to the **Typhoon Banking Platform**. This guide will walk you through the core features of your digital banking experience, whether you are a retail customer, a corporate client, or an administrator.

---

## 1. Getting Started & Onboarding

### Account Registration & KYC (Know Your Customer)
Before you can send or receive funds, you must verify your identity to comply with banking regulations.
1. **Sign Up:** Create an account using your email and a secure password.
2. **Profile Completion:** Enter your personal details (name, address, date of birth).
3. **Identity Verification:** Navigate to the **Compliance/KYC** section in your dashboard. You will be prompted to upload a government-issued ID (Passport, National ID) and proof of address, or use our video identification partner.
4. **Account Activation:** Once a Compliance Officer approves your documents (or automated verification succeeds), your account will be activated, and an IBAN will be assigned to you.

---

## 2. Managing Your Accounts

### Dashboards
- **Retail Dashboard:** Gives you an overview of your total balance, recent transactions, and portfolio distribution (fiat vs. crypto).
- **Corporate Portal:** For business users, offering multi-user access controls, bulk payment capabilities, and API key management.

### Finding Your IBAN & SWIFT
To receive money, you need to share your banking details.
1. Go to **Accounts** > **Details**.
2. Here you will find your **Account Name**, **IBAN** (International Bank Account Number), and **BIC/SWIFT** code.

---

## 3. Payments & Transfers

The platform supports multiple ways to move money. Navigate to the **Transfers** tab to initiate a payment.

### Internal Transfers
Send money instantly to other users on the Typhoon platform.
* Select **Internal Transfer**.
* Enter the recipient's email or Typhoon Account ID.
* Funds settle immediately with zero fees.

### SEPA Credit Transfers (Europe)
Send Euros to any bank account within the Single Euro Payments Area.
* Select **SEPA Transfer**.
* Enter the recipient's Name and IBAN.
* Standard transfers take 1-2 business days. *(Instant SEPA is subject to availability).*

### SWIFT & TARGET2 Transfers (International)
For cross-border payments outside of SEPA.
* Select **International (SWIFT)**.
* You will need the recipient's Name, Account Number/IBAN, and their bank's BIC/SWIFT code.
* *Note: Intermediate bank fees may apply depending on your fee schedule.*

---

## 4. Crypto Exchange

Typhoon allows you to trade cryptocurrencies directly from your bank account balance.

1. Go to the **Crypto** or **Exchange** tab.
2. **Buy/Sell:** Choose the fiat currency to spend and the cryptocurrency to buy (e.g., EUR to BTC).
3. **Order Types:** 
   - **Market Order:** Buy immediately at the current market exchange rate.
   - **Limit Order:** Set a specific price at which you want the system to buy or sell.
4. **Wallets:** Purchased crypto is stored in your secure Typhoon Crypto Wallet. You can view your portfolio performance on your main dashboard.

---

## 5. Corporate Features

If you are a registered business, the **Corporate Portal** offers advanced tools:
* **Bulk Payments:** Upload a CSV file of IBANs and amounts (e.g., for payroll) to process hundreds of SEPA transfers at once.
* **Role Management:** Invite your team members (Accountants, Managers) and set specific permissions (e.g., view-only vs. ability to execute payments).
* **API Access:** Generate API credentials to connect your ERP or accounting software directly to your Typhoon account.

---

## 6. Security & Support

* **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Highly recommended. Go to **Settings > Security** to enable authenticator app (TOTP) codes for logins and large transfers.
* **Transaction Monitoring:** Our system automatically monitors for suspicious activity. If a transaction is flagged, it may be temporarily held pending review by our Compliance Team.
* **Alerts:** Set up email notifications for incoming transfers or successful crypto orders in **Settings > Notifications**.

If you need help or your account is restricted, please contact our support team through the **Help** section in your dashboard.
